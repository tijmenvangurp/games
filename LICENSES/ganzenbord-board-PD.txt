Ganzenbord board
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oud_Hollands_Ganzenbord,_objectnr_A_41258.tif

The board in apps/games/public/ganzenbord/board.webp is a cropped and
resized version of "Oud Hollands Ganzenbord, objectnr A 41258" from the
Rijksmuseum collection, published on Wikimedia Commons.
The work is in the public domain.

The scan was cropped to the printed board, resized to 1800 px wide and
saved as WebP. The layout, numbers and illustrations are unchanged.
